# SoulAlcove

**English** | [Русский](./README.ru.md)

> A private Android mood diary that never leaves your phone.

SoulAlcove is a minimal mood tracker and emotional journal for Android. No account, no cloud, no ad tracking — just you and your day.

## Features

- **One-tap mood logging** — pick a color, done.
- **Short notes** — attach a thought to any entry when you want to.
- **Day-by-day history** — browse your emotional timeline in a clean, quiet interface.
- **100% local** — all data is stored on your device using SQLite. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Why SoulAlcove

- **Private by design** — your emotional data never leaves your phone.
- **No registration** — open the app and start immediately.
- **Offline-first** — works fully without an internet connection.
- **Lightweight** — no bloat, no unnecessary permissions.

## Tech Stack

- **Python** + **Flet** / **KivyMD** (Android GUI)
- **SQLite** for local storage
- No backend, no external servers

## Status

First public Android release. Core experience is ready: fast logging, readable history, full local privacy.

Planned for future updates: insights, gentle streaks, and better ways to look back at your emotional patterns — always staying local-first.
